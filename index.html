A cozy meetup for all game makers

An informal evening for the Tel Aviv game dev scene. No talks, no slides, no pitch competition. Just a bar, a crowd of people who make games, and time to actually talk to each other.

Entry is free, you buy your own drinks (if you want), and you can drop in whenever suits you. RSVP if you like or just show up, and feel free to bring friends. Got a build on your phone or laptop? Bring it and show it off.

Who comes

  • Programmers
  • Designers
  • Artists
  • Animators
  • Producers
  • Audio
  • QA
  • Community & marketing
  • Students
  • Indies
  • Studio folks
  • Curious newcomers

Mobile, PC, console, VR, jam games. If you make games or want to, you're in.
